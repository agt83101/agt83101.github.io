PRIVACY POLICY

Derelict Shift · Last updated 21 November 2026

In short: Derelict Shift has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details. Anonymous usage analytics (Google Analytics) are off unless you choose to allow them. Your progress, scores and settings are saved on your own device only.

1. Who we are

Derelict Shift is a daily browser game made and operated by Andrew Gavin Taylor (andrewgtaylor.design). In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the operator of the game at derelictshift.app.

Contact us using this contact form

2. Anonymous usage analytics (only if you allow it)

The first time you open the game it asks whether you allow anonymous analytics. Nothing is sent to Google Analytics unless you tap ALLOW. If you tap NO THANKS (or ignore the banner), analytics never load.

If you allow analytics, we use Google Analytics, provided through Google Tag Manager, to understand how the game is used so we can improve it. This may include:

  • your device type, operating system, browser and screen size;
  • your approximate location (such as country or city), worked out from your network address;
  • how you use the game: pages and screens viewed, how long you stay, and which buttons or features are used;
  • a random identifier stored in a cookie or similar technology, so repeat visits can be counted without knowing who you are.

Google processes this data on our behalf and under its own terms. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. We do not use analytics data to identify you, and we do not use it for advertising.

Analytics data is kept for 2 months and then deleted automatically.

3. What we do not collect

  • No accounts, usernames or passwords.
  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses.
  • No payment details.
  • No precise location and no contact lists.
  • Your game scores are not sent to us. They are calculated and stored on your device.

4. What is saved on your device

The game uses your browser's local storage to remember things between visits. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

WhatWhy
Today's progress, deaths, hides and bonusesTo keep your daily score, lock completed levels and show your results.
Streak and average-score historyTo show your stats.
Your analytics choiceSo we do not ask you again.
Your "skip Objectives and Tips" settingTo remember your preference.

Clearing your browser data, or using a different browser or device, resets all of this.

5. Your choices

  • Change your mind: tap the Privacy button (shield icon) at the top of the game, then choose ALLOW or DECLINE. Declining stops analytics from your next visit; any data already collected stays until it expires under the retention period above.
  • Block it in your browser: you can block cookies and trackers in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Declining does not affect play. Scoring, daily levels and sharing work exactly the same.

6. Sharing your results

The "Transmit status" button creates a short text summary of your result (for example, your score and how often you were caught). It is only shared if you choose to send it, and only to wherever you choose to send it. You can edit it first. We do not receive it.

7. Other parties

Besides Google (analytics, only with your permission), our web hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address and the pages requested, for security and reliability. The game itself does not load advertising or social-media trackers.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to correct or delete it, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because analytics data is anonymous and we have no account for you, we usually cannot link it to a particular person, but you can contact us and we will help where we can. You may also complain to your local data protection or privacy regulator.

9. Children

Derelict Shift is a general-audience game with no accounts. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game uses data, we will update this page and the date at the top, and ask again for your permission if the change affects analytics.
